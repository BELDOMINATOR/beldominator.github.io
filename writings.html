I HATE GOING BACK

August 23, 2026

I'm SO pissed off and sad, I DO NOT want to go back to the country where I live. I want to STAY HERE in the country where I was born. I cry every time I have to go back. Every year it's the same: 10 months locked in that school and then 3 months vacation, and I only get ONE month in the country where I was born. One. Sometimes a little more if I'm lucky.

Just thinking that in a few weeks school starts again makes me furious and miserable. I can't stand it anymore. School crushes me, I hate being stuck around all those people.

I want to be here with my REAL family. MY REAL FAMILY. I WAS BORN HERE. If it wasn't for my dad I WOULD BE LIVING HERE.

Everyone at school hates me and ignores me because of ONE girl. We were best friends since preschool in 2014, same class every year until 8th grade. Now she's in my school, thank god not in my class. After our fight in 6th grade she started spreading lies about me and now she's miss popular and of course everyone hates me. I lost EVERY friend from elementary because of her. I lost ALL my middle school friends. And now? I talk to ONE girl in my class. ONE.

I love it here, here I actually forget how much I hate people because my family here treats me right, they actually care if I'm happy. They WANT me to be happy and that means everything to me. I don't want to go to school. I'm exhausted. I'm done.

And now the best part: my school is too full so they made another school and some classes have to move. GUESS WHO THEY PICKED? ME. MY CLASS. They could have picked ANY other class but nooo, they had to pick MINE! Every parent is furious at the principal because she won't do anything and refuses to cooperate.
